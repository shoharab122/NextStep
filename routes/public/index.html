Student Recruitment – Cost List by NSI
STUDY IN MALAYSIA – ESTIMATED COST LIST FOR BANGLADESHI STUDENTS (NextStep Immigration)

Estimated Costs

  1. EMGS Fees: RM 2,800 – 3,800 – Paid after the student proceeds with the application. Exact amount depends on the university and the student's situation.
  2. EVAL / Visa-Related Fee: RM 159 – Charges can change, so always confirm the current university / EMGS quotation before giving a final amount.
  3. Initial Tuition & University Fees: per university quote – Depends on university, programme, major, intake and any scholarship or discount. Includes 1st semester tuition, registration fee, caution / deposit, international admission fee and other university fees.
  4. Flight Ticket (One-Way): BDT 34,000 – 60,000 – Bangladesh to Malaysia. Varies by booking date, airline, travel date, season and baggage allowance.

Total Cost Formula

EMGS Fees + EVAL Fee + Initial Tuition & University Fees + Flight Ticket

There is no single fixed amount for every student. The final cost is calculated according to the student's chosen university and programme.

All amounts are estimates. Please confirm the current university / EMGS quotation before giving a final amount.
